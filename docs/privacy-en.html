Privacy policy

MAKGrib · updated 14 September 2026

In short: the app collects nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere. Your position is determined on the device and stays there.

What this is

MAKGrib is a free, non-commercial, open-source application for viewing marine weather forecasts. It has no accounts, no registration and no advertising.

Data collected

None. The app collects no personal or device data, stores none of it anywhere outside your device, and shares none with third parties. It contains no analytics and no advertising libraries.

Location

If you grant location access, the app shows your position on the map. Coordinates are computed on the device, used only for display, and are never transmitted — neither to the developer nor to anyone else. No track history is kept. The permission is optional: everything else works without it.

Network use

The app goes online only when you tap “download weather”. The request goes directly to the public servers of the United States National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA/NCEP, nomads.ncep.noaa.gov). It carries only the bounds of the map area and the forecast parameters — how many days and at what interval. It carries nothing about you, your device or your position.

What NOAA does with such requests is governed by that organisation; the developer of MAKGrib has no connection to their servers.

Children

The app is made for skippers and fishermen and is not directed at children. It collects no age information.

Not a navigation device

MAKGrib displays weather forecasts and helps in planning a passage. It is not a navigation instrument and not a substitute for proper equipment, charts or official storm warnings. The decision to put to sea rests with the skipper.
